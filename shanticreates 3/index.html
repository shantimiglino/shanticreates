About

I’m Shanti, a visual storyteller. Photography and video are how I explore the world, meet people and make sense of the places I find myself in.

Traveling has shaped who I am and how I see. Being somewhere new reminds me to slow down, listen and look past my first impression. Wherever I go, I want to understand what a place means to the people who call it home, and my camera is often what starts that conversation.

I want to stay curious and keep learning: about people, about places and about my own craft. Every project teaches me a new way of seeing.

Most of all, I want to deliver people’s stories in a creative way. I look for the small details, a gesture, a routine, the way light falls, and shape them into images that feel honest and personal. I hope my work invites the same curiosity in you: Who is this person? What matters to them? What might we share?
